Calm, colorful puzzle games

Bekuiz Games is a small independent studio making puzzle games for iPhone. Our first game, Paintbloom, is coming soon: mix paint drops in little bowls and color in 100 paintings, a new one in every level. No lives, no energy, and it plays offline. Contact: bekuiz.games@gmail.com
