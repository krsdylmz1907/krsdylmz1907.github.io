Calm, colorful puzzle games

Bekuiz Games is the independent game brand of Kürşad Yılmaz, making calm puzzle games for iPhone. Our first game is Paintbloom: mix paint drops in little bowls and color in 100 paintings, a new one in every level. No lives, no energy, and it plays offline. We also make Special Day, which remembers anniversaries and birthdays for you and goes quiet once you've got the gift (Support · Privacy). Contact: bekuiz.games@gmail.com
